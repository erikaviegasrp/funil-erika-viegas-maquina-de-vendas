# Funil Érika Viegas — Playbook de Vendas

Use these as directional planning hypotheses, not promises. Actual results depend on audience, budget, auction, creative quality, landing page, offer, frequency and tracking.

## Stage matrix

| Stage | Job | Message territory | Primary action | Useful metrics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Topo | Create recognition | Culture, prevention, daily choices, observation | View, save, share, visit | Reach, frequency, video view rate, 3-second/25% view rate, saves, shares, CTR |
| Meio | Build understanding | Checklist, method, documents, events, routines | Read, watch, compare, request checklist | Landing-page view rate, engaged sessions, carousel completion, CTR, content downloads, cost per engaged visit |
| Fundo | Convert concern into action | Conditional risk, review, technical support, pre-diagnosis | Lead, call, WhatsApp, scheduled assessment | Qualified lead rate, form completion, cost per lead, contact rate, booked assessment rate |

## Directional ranges for UI labels

Show a range with an explicit assumption, for example: `Faixa de planejamento · CTR 0,6–1,2% · hipótese inicial`.

| Format | Primary metric | Directional range | Assumption |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Instagram Feed / Stories | CTR | 0,6–1,4% | Cold B2B audience, clear visual hook, traffic objective |
| Instagram Reels / Shorts | 3-second view rate | 25–45% | Native vertical motion, first-second hook |
| Instagram Carousel | Completion rate | 15–30% | 3–5 cards, progressive narrative |
| Google Search | CTR | 3–8% | High-intent non-brand queries and relevant ad group |
| Google Display | CTR | 0,2–0,7% | Prospecting or remarketing with strong creative/context |
| Google Demand Gen | CTR | 0,5–1,2% | Visual-first creative and qualified audience signal |
| YouTube in-feed | View rate | 8–20% | Thumbnail and title aligned to an educational promise |
| Lead form / pre-diagnosis | Completion rate | 8–20% | Short form, clear offer, mobile-friendly page |

## KPI copy pattern

Use this compact card structure beside a format:

- **Objetivo:** what the asset is expected to move.
- **Métrica principal:** the single leading indicator.
- **Faixa de planejamento:** a range, never a guarantee.
- **Hipótese:** why the format may perform in this stage.
- **Cuidado:** what should not be inferred from the number.

## Example metadata

```ts
kpi: {
  primary: "CTR",
  secondary: "Salvamentos",
  estimate: "0,6–1,4%",
  assumption: "Audiência fria B2B; criativo com gancho claro",
  caution: "Faixa inicial de planejamento, não promessa de performance",
}
```

## Presentation guidance

Present the funnel in order: **Percepção → Clareza → Ação**. Let the client move from the architecture card to the corresponding gallery, then open an asset detail to see channel, placement and KPI hypothesis. Keep legal notes and measurement assumptions visible but secondary to the story.
